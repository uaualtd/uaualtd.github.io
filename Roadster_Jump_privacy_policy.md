# Roadster Jump — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

>

Roadster Jump is a driving game. This policy explains what information Roadster Jump handles, what
it does not collect, and where your data is kept.

## Overview

Roadster Jump runs entirely on your device.

- No user accounts and no sign-in.
- No advertising, no advertising SDKs and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
- No analytics and no crash reporting.
- No developer-operated server that receives any of your data.

## Information we collect

Roadster Jump does not collect any personal information. It does not ask for your name, email
address, phone number, location, contacts or photos, and it sends nothing about you or how you play
to the developer or to anyone else.

## On-device storage

Roadster Jump stores a small amount of data locally on your device:

- Your settings, such as sound, music, graphics quality and the Roadster's paint.
- Your progress: lights and gems earned, rank and goals, your daily streak, best chase scores and
  driving stats.
- A record of whether you hold Roadster Pro, so it works between launches. It is checked again
  with the App Store or Google Play each time the app opens.

This data never leaves your device. If you uninstall Roadster Jump, it is removed according to your
platform's normal behaviour (it may be kept in your device's own backups, under your backup
settings).

## Sharing

If you choose to share a postcard of a drive, the picture is created on your device and handed to
your device's own share sheet. It goes only where you send it. Roadster Jump does not upload it or
keep a copy beyond your device's temporary storage.

## Notifications

If you allow notifications, Roadster Jump uses them for one thing: telling you when a timed
Auto-drive session has ended while the app is in the background. They are scheduled locally on your
device. No notification content leaves the device and there is no push server.

## In-app purchases

Roadster Jump offers Roadster Pro as an optional in-app purchase: an auto-renewing monthly
subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase.

- Payment is processed by the App Store or Google Play, not by the developer.
- Roadster Jump receives only the purchase status needed to unlock and restore Roadster Pro.
- Roadster Jump never receives your payment card details.
- Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in your App Store or Google Play account settings.
- Your purchases are governed by Apple's or Google's own terms and privacy policies.

## Network access

The game itself plays offline. Network activity happens only for platform-managed reasons, such as
in-app purchase and restore checks through Apple or Google, and opening a link you tapped. The app
may also download a small settings file that tunes graphics quality for particular phone models; this
request carries no personal information. Roadster Jump runs no backend of its own.

## Third-party services

Roadster Jump uses only the platform and framework components it needs to run, such as the Flutter
runtime and the store billing libraries. It includes no advertising, analytics or cross-app tracking
SDKs. Where platform services are involved, those providers handle data under their own policies.

## Children's privacy

Roadster Jump is a general-audience game and is not directed at children. It collects no personal
data from anyone, so it does not knowingly collect personal data from children. Parents and
guardians can remove all app data by clearing app data or uninstalling Roadster Jump.

## Your control over your data

Because Roadster Jump's data lives on your device:

- You can clear local app data from your device settings.
- You can uninstall Roadster Jump to remove it, subject to your device backup settings.
- You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

## Security

Roadster Jump relies on the technical measures the platform and app framework provide for local
data. No software is perfectly secure, and device-level protections, such as your passcode, OS
updates and backup settings, remain your responsibility.

## Changes to this policy

If Roadster Jump's data practices change, this policy is updated and the date at the top of this
page changes with it.
