# Privacy Policy for TelePro

**Last Updated: October 30, 2026**

---

## Introduction

TelePro ("the App") is a toolkit for your Telegram account. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

**The short version: we don't collect, store, or share your data.**

---

## What We Collect

Nothing. We do not collect personal information, and we do not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting services. We do not run servers that receive your data.

---

## Telegram

The App connects directly to Telegram using Telegram's official library. You sign in with your own Telegram account, and your messages, media, and account data travel only between your device and Telegram. They are never sent to us.

Data held by Telegram is covered by Telegram's privacy policy: https://telegram.org/privacy

---

## Data on Your Device

Anything the App keeps, such as your Telegram session, cached chats, files you export or download, and your settings, is stored only on your device. Logging out ends your Telegram session, and uninstalling the App removes its data. Files leave your device only when you choose to share them.

The App may load general resources, such as fonts, from standard content services. These requests contain no personal or Telegram data.

---

## Permissions

The App asks for device permissions, such as storage or file access, only when a feature needs them, and uses them only on your device.

---

## Purchases

Optional premium features are sold through the App Store or Google Play. Payments are handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details.

---

## Children's Privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

---

## Security

Your connection to Telegram is encrypted by Telegram's own protocol, and sensitive data on your device is kept in the platform's secure storage.

---

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted here, with a new "Last Updated" date.
