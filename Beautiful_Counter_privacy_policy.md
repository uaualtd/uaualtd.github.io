# Beautiful Counter — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Beautiful Counter is a tally counter and habit tracker. This policy explains what it handles and
where your data is kept.

## Overview

Beautiful Counter runs entirely on your device.

- No user accounts and no sign-in.
- No advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking.
- No server: the app makes no network connections.

## Information we collect

None. Beautiful Counter does not collect any personal information and sends nothing about you or
how you use it to the developer or anyone else.

## On-device storage

Beautiful Counter stores on your device only:

- Your items (their names, colours and targets) and the count for each day.
- Your settings, such as the scene, haptics and whether the volume buttons count.

This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app removes it, subject to your device's own
backup settings.

## Volume buttons

While the counter is on screen, the app listens for the volume buttons so they can add or take away
a count. It does not record audio or use the microphone.

## Children's privacy

Beautiful Counter is a general-audience app. It collects no personal data from anyone, including
children.

## Your control over your data

You can reset any item's counts in the app, delete items, clear the app's data in your device
settings, or uninstall it.

## Changes to this policy

If Beautiful Counter's data practices change, this policy is updated and the date above changes
with it.
