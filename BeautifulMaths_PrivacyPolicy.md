Beautiful Maths is a mental arithmetic trainer. This policy explains what information Beautiful
Maths handles, what it does not collect, and where your data is kept.

## Overview

Beautiful Maths runs entirely on your device.

- No user accounts and no sign-in.
- No advertising SDKs.
- No analytics or tracking SDKs.
- No in-app purchases.
- No internet access: the app does not send or receive data over the network.

## Information we collect

Beautiful Maths does not collect personal data. Nothing you do in the app is sent to the developer
or to anyone else, including:

- Your name, email address, phone number or contact details.
- Your answers, scores, progress or settings.
- Usage analytics or behavioural tracking data.

## On-device storage

Beautiful Maths stores a small amount of data locally on your device so you can pick up where you
left off:

- Your progress: stars, XP and level, rating, best scores, problems solved and your daily streak.
- Your settings: sound and haptics on or off.

If you uninstall Beautiful Maths, this local data is removed according to your platform's normal
behaviour. Your device's own backup (iCloud or Google) may include it, under Apple's or Google's
backup settings and policies.

## Permissions

Beautiful Maths asks for no permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone, location,
contacts, photos or files. Sounds and vibrations are output only.

## Third-party services

Beautiful Maths is built with the Flutter and Flame frameworks and uses standard on-device
components for sound and saving progress. None of them send your data anywhere. It includes no
third-party advertising or user-tracking SDKs.

## Children's privacy

Beautiful Maths collects no personal data from anyone, including children. It shows no ads, has no
purchases, no accounts and no way to contact other people. Parents and guardians can remove all app
data with Settings → Reset progress, by clearing app data, or by uninstalling the app.

## Your control over your data

Because Beautiful Maths's data lives on your device:

- You can clear your progress at any time with Settings → Reset progress.
- You can clear local app data from your device settings.
- You can uninstall Beautiful Maths to remove it, subject to your device backup settings.

## Security

Beautiful Maths relies on the technical measures the platform and app framework provide for local
data. No software is perfectly secure, and device-level protections — passcode, biometrics, OS
updates and backup settings — remain your responsibility.

## Changes to this policy

If Beautiful Maths's data practices change, this policy is updated and the date at the top of this
page changes with it.

## Contact

For privacy questions about Beautiful Maths, write to uaua.ltd+beautifulmaths@gmail.com.
